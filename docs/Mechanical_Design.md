# Mechanical Design

## Chassis

- **Material:** Steel tubular (25 mm × 25 mm × 2 mm square tube)
- **Fabrication:** MIG welding
- **Length:** ~1.8 m
- **Width:** ~0.9 m
- **Wheelbase:** ~1.2 m
- **Total weight (empty):** ~90 kg

## Front Suspension

- **Type:** Telescopic fork with shock absorbers
- **Travel:** ~80 mm
- **Steering:** Handlebar with direct linkage

## Rear Axle

- **Type:** Solid axle with differential (or single-motor direct drive)
- **Wheels:** 2× rear wheels
- **Braking:** Mechanical drum brake

## Safety

- Front and rear lights
- Horn
- Emergency stop switch
- Hand brake

## Assembly Sequence

1. Cut steel tubes to length
2. Weld chassis frame (MIG)
3. Assemble front suspension
4. Mount rear axle and wheels
5. Install motor and controller
6. Wire electrical system
7. Mount HMI display
8. Test and commission
